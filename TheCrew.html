Meet the Studio Chibi Crew!

Studio Chibi consists of content creators, all from different backgrounds of the Sky community and from different parts of the world. From the UK to Australia! From meme creators to tutorial specialists, they are all some of the largest influencers in the community.

XK Generations

XK Generations is a UK-based content creator best known for videos about Sky: Children of the Light. His most popular uploads include Cosmetics Stereotypes, Sky Rewind 2020, as well as a variety of meme and tutorial content. Over the years, XK has collaborated with many leading Sky YouTubers such as Fox Band (also known as thatskyfox), Toyz Gaming, Emptea, and BluSky. In addition to Sky, he also creates Minecraft videos with friends and produces Nintendo-related content.
